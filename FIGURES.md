# Figures

The page's figures are Voltaire's 2025 artwork, placed as the original vectors rather than redrawn. They are Voltaire's, and are not covered by this repository's license.

- **Dark theme:** `assets/figures/<name>.svg`, each the 2025 source file byte for byte, except the two charts below. The sources are drawn in what is already the design system's dark palette: lavender `#D4C6E1` is its dark-theme text color, and `#0A090C` its dark ground.
- **Light theme:** `assets/figures/light/<name>.svg`, the same file with only the colors in the last column changed. Geometry, lettering and composition are untouched. The light colors are design-system-ASK's own light-theme diagram colors: ink `#6A637F` (`--fg-1`), node fill `#BFB3D4` (`--ask-surface`), and the light ground, `#D4C6E1` to `#E2D3F0`. Three figures also change colors inside an embedded raster, with its alpha and size unchanged: `how-it-works-chart.svg` is an SVG that wraps one raster chart, `why-now-futures.svg` draws its two areas as a raster, and `tokenized-vaults.svg` carries raster knockouts behind its labels.
- **Pairing:** `styles/voltaire.css` shows one file of each pair, on the same conditions as the shell's light and dark identity mark.

To replace a figure, put the new file at `assets/figures/<name>.svg`, write its light-theme twin at `assets/figures/light/<name>.svg`, and keep both `<img>` elements in `index.html`. A figure that works on both grounds needs only one `<img>`, without a pairing class.
